# Visual / Mobile audit - amiradecocarpets.be
Tested: Chromium, desktop 1440x900, iPhone 12 (390x664 viewport), DSF 1. Sandbox was on.
Screenshots: /home/user/business-website-template/amiradecocarpets.be-audit/screenshots/ (desktop|mobile)-(home-early|home|home-after-consent|category).png

## Issues
- HIGH: Cookie consent modal covers the whole above-the-fold area on first load. Desktop: ~750x315 centered modal plus dark overlay. Mobile: ~90% of viewport, 3 stacked buttons, hero, H1 and CTA fully hidden. Fix: use a compact bottom banner or small modal with short text and equal-weight Weigeren/Toestaan buttons. Do not block the hero. Move the long text to the "Over cookies" tab.
- HIGH: The CTA "Ontdek ons assortiment!" is a thin underlined text link (1140x24 box on desktop, 360x37 on mobile) laid over a busy photo. It sits at the left edge, off-center from the centered headline. Contrast is poor (on the desktop grass photo it is nearly unreadable). Fix: make it a solid filled button (min 48px high), centered under the headline, with a contrasting colour. Add a 40-50% dark overlay to the hero image.
- HIGH: Value proposition is mixed. The hero says "Sfeer en comfort voor ieder interieur" over a KUNSTGRAS (artificial grass) photo on desktop and a living room on mobile. Slides rotate. The brand name is in the eyebrow. There is no mention of the product type (tapijten/kunstgras), no USP (price, delivery, showroom) and no phone/order CTA. Fix: state "Tapijten en kunstgras in Wetteren" with 2-3 USPs and two CTAs (Shop / Bel ons).
- MEDIUM: The hero H1 renders readably visually (the "||" separators are not visible). But there are 3 H1s in the DOM. The first is empty. The other two are hidden/duplicated, with textContent "Ontdek de beste tapijtenwinkel ... || Amira Deco-|| Carpets", and sit far down the page (y~2600 / 3400 on mobile). The visible hero headline is NOT an H1. Fix: one H1 in the hero ("Tapijten en kunstgras in Wetteren"); remove the "||" markup and the empty H1.
- MEDIUM: Broken or missing assets. Early desktop load shows a broken-image icon and unstyled phone links and a Facebook icon at the top-left (0,120-190), over the hero. The ".be" site returns 404 for /smarty/webshop02/media/images/{badge1,badge3,h1,left-content,btn}.png and webshop04/sep.png. It returned 502 for icon-close.png and btn.png on mobile. Fix: remove or repair these CSS/theme references. Make the social/phone bar either properly positioned or hidden.
- MEDIUM: Mobile header. The nav bar is an empty white strip with the hamburger centered at (195,190). The burger is in the middle of an empty bar, not aligned with the logo or icons. The header takes ~220px of the 664px viewport. Fix: put the burger in the same row as the logo and icons and add an aria-label. Reduce the header height.
- MEDIUM: Tap targets. 44-50 interactive elements are under 44px. Desktop: the main nav links are 14px high (HOME 44x14, TAPIJTEN 61x14), phone links 22px, "Lees meer" 65x22, header icons 38-40px, cookie settings gear 30x30. Mobile: the menu links are 260x30, icons 45x36, "Registreren/Vergeten" 22px high. Fix: padding so every target is at least 44x44 (WCAG 2.5.8 requires 24px, Google recommends 48px). Set a min gap of 8px.
- MEDIUM: Layout shift visible during load. Measured CLS about 0.023 (good, below 0.1) but there are visible changes. The hero is a dark placeholder at 0.8s, then the image appears at around 3s. The social icons and phone links appear unstyled and then reposition. Fix: set explicit hero dimensions and a background colour, preload the hero LCP image, and inline critical CSS for the top bar.
- LOW: Font sizes. Body is 15px (96 text nodes on mobile), with 12px (7 nodes) and 14px (13) also present. Fix: base 16px on mobile, with 14px minimum for captions.
- LOW: Fixed bottom-left cookie gear (30x30) and the "online" Proxi logo at bottom-right overlap the content strip ("Tapijten" heading) at the fold on both devices. Fix: keep them clear of content or make them smaller.
- LOW: 6 of 16 images lack alt (known). Add descriptive alt text, empty alt for decorative images.
- LOW: The category page /nl/tapijten-2 opens with the same hero (stale image on mobile, the grass photo shown for carpets). H1 "Ontdek de mooiste tapijten voor uw interieurdecoratie" is below the hero. Fix: use a carpet-specific image and show the H1 in the hero.

## Passed
- No horizontal overflow (scrollWidth equals viewport on both devices, home and category).
- The hero text renders readably (white serif, ~45px desktop / 24px mobile) once consent is dismissed. Contrast is acceptable on the living-room photo but weaker on the grass photo.
- Logo, search, account, wishlist and cart are visible above the fold. Address is shown in the header.
